01 / Introduction

About this portfolio

One place to document the skills I have developed, the music that inspires me, and the projects I have worked to bring to life.

This portfolio began as a web development project. Over time, it grew into something more personal: a space where I could combine technical learning with my interest in classical music and my development as a pianist.

Web development taught me to plan, build, test, and refine. Music gave the project its subject and direction. Bringing them together has made the website both a practical demonstration of my coding skills and a record of the work that motivates me.

02 / The process

Development timeline

From foundational CSS lessons to a published, interactive portfolio. Each stage built on the skills introduced before it.

Stage 01

Building layout foundations

I explored Flexbox, the main and cross axes, alignment, wrapping, growing, and CSS positioning. I then studied responsive design, media queries, transitions, and hover effects, learning to create layouts that adapt to different screens.

FlexboxPositioningResponsive CSSTransitions
Stage 02

First CSS mini project

I combined colour, typography, spacing, layout, and responsive techniques to create a landing page. This helped me think about visual consistency and how individual design decisions work together.

Landing pageVisual design
Stage 03

Learning JavaScript

I moved from static pages to programming concepts: variables, data types, operators, conditions, loops, functions, and arrays. I then learned DOM selection, text and class changes, and event handling to make webpages respond to users.

JavaScriptDOMEvents
Stage 04

Planning and building a portfolio

I created wireframes, planned the content and assets, and built the HTML structure. I then developed the visual design with CSS and added interactive features, bringing the three core web technologies together in one project.

WireframesSemantic HTMLProject planning
Stage 05

Turning the portfolio towards music

I expanded the site's purpose by adding research into Frédéric Chopin and a section about my own experience as a pianist. I organised the research, gathered images, and considered how performance recordings and multimedia could fit into the site.

ResearchContent designMultimedia
Stage 06

Refining the experience

I experimented with Tailwind CSS, improved the layout and navigation, and built a responsive hamburger menu with Font Awesome icons. I used JavaScript event listeners and class toggling to control the menu and added a theme switch using CSS variables and browser storage.

Tailwind CSSMobile navigationDark mode
Stage 07

Testing, sharing, and presenting

I explored connecting a contact form through external services, worked through issues with embedded videos and GitHub hosting, and made final adjustments. I presented the portfolio to parents and teachers, then exchanged feedback and website reviews with a friend.

TestingGitHubPresentationPeer feedback
03 / Musical research

Frédéric Chopin

Exploring the composer whose music helped shape the direction of this portfolio.

The Romantic piano
Technical precision and expressive freedom are not opposites; they can deepen one another.
A theme explored through Chopin's music

A lasting influence on piano music

My research into Frédéric Chopin began with his biography, musical development, and place in the Romantic period. I wanted to understand not only his historical significance, but also why his compositions continue to interest pianists and listeners.

I then focused on his études and nocturnes, examining the relationship between technical difficulty and musical expression. His works show how demanding piano writing can also communicate atmosphere, character, and emotion.

This section brings together historical research and my own perspective as a pianist who admires his compositions.

04 / Personal development

My piano journey

A developing relationship with the instrument, shaped by practice, difficult repertoire, listening, and performance.

Learning beyond the notes

Studying Chopin also encouraged me to reflect on my own development as a pianist. Learning challenging compositions has involved more than memorising notes: it has required attention to technique, musical structure, voicing, texture, and interpretation. I want this portfolio to document that process honestly, including both progress and the areas that still need work.

  • Documenting repertoire and learning experiences
  • Reflecting on practice and technical challenges
  • Exploring musical expression and interpretation
  • Sharing recordings and performance material
  • Connecting personal progress with musical research
